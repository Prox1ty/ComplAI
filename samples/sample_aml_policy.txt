ACME BANK LIMITED
ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING AND KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER POLICY
Version 0.3 (Draft for Board review)

1. Purpose
1.1 This policy sets out how Acme Bank Limited identifies, assesses and mitigates the risk that its products, services and delivery channels are used for money laundering or terrorist financing.

2. Definitions
2.1 "Customer" means any natural or legal person who maintains an account with the Bank or who conducts an occasional transaction through any branch or digital channel of the Bank.
2.2 "Beneficial owner" means the natural person who ultimately owns or controls a customer, or on whose behalf a transaction is being conducted.

3. Customer Due Diligence
3.1 The Bank will verify the identity of each customer using reliable, independent source documents, data or information before or during the course of establishing a business relationship.
3.2 Customer identification records may be verified within 90 days after the account is opened where the branch manager considers it appropriate to do so.
3.3 The Bank will identify the beneficial owners of legal persons only where a single natural person holds more than 50 percent of the ownership interest.
3.4 Enhanced due diligence may be applied to customers that staff consider to be higher risk, based on their own judgement and experience.

4. Politically Exposed Persons
4.1 Accounts for politically exposed persons may be opened by the relationship manager after completing the standard customer due diligence process.

5. Ongoing Monitoring
5.1 The Bank will review customer profiles and supporting documents periodically as resources permit.
5.2 Transactions that appear unusual or inconsistent with the customer profile should be discussed with the line manager before any further action is taken.

6. Record Keeping
6.1 The Bank will retain records of all domestic and international transactions for at least three years after the date of the transaction.

7. Reporting
7.1 Suspicious transactions shall be reported to the Compliance Officer, who will decide whether to inform the Financial Monitoring Unit within a reasonable time.

8. Training
8.1 All staff will receive anti-money laundering training at the time of joining the Bank.

9. Governance
9.1 This policy will be approved by the Chief Executive Officer and reviewed whenever management considers it necessary.
